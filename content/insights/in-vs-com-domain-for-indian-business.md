If your customers are mainly in India, a **.in domain is an excellent choice**: it's India's own country domain, it tells Google and customers you're an Indian business, and good names are easier to find than on .com. If you sell internationally or plan to, **.com** is more widely recognised abroad. Both are trusted and both can rank well on Google. The best domain is the one that's **short, easy to say on the phone and matches your business name**.

We get asked this on almost every new website project in Surat, so here's the full answer.

## What .in, .co.in and .com actually mean

- **.in** is India's country code domain, managed by the National Internet Exchange of India ([NIXI](https://registry.in/)).
- **.co.in** is a second-level option under .in, traditionally used by companies.
- **.com** is the original "commercial" domain, used worldwide.

There are others (.net, .biz, .online and many new ones), but for most businesses the real choice is between .in and .com.

## How the domain affects Google rankings

Google treats country code domains like .in as a signal that a site is relevant to India. That can help for searches made in India. A .com doesn't carry a country signal, so Google relies on other clues (your content, address, language and links) to understand where you serve.

In practice, the **extension is a small factor**. Your content, your Google Business Profile, your reviews and your links matter far more. A .com business in Surat can rank well locally, and a .in business can still sell abroad. The domain won't make or break your SEO.

## Which one should you choose?

| Your situation | Best choice |
| --- | --- |
| Customers mainly in India, especially local | .in |
| Selling across India and abroad | .com, or both |
| Exporting or targeting international buyers | .com |
| Your exact name is taken on .com | .in (better than a long or hyphenated .com) |
| A company that wants a formal Indian identity | .in or .co.in |

Our own main site is magolabs.in because our core market is India, and we use a separate site for overseas clients. That split works well when your audiences are clearly different.

## Should you buy both?

If your budget allows, **buy both the .in and .com of your name** and point one to the other. It protects your brand, stops someone else using your name, and catches people who type the wrong one. Choose one as your main address and use it everywhere.

## How to choose a good domain name

- **Use your business name** or the shortest clear version of it
- **Keep it short** and easy to spell when said aloud on a call
- **Avoid hyphens and numbers**, which people get wrong
- **Don't stuff keywords** ("best-website-design-surat.in" looks spammy and doesn't help much)
- **Check social media handles** at the same time so they match
- **Register it in your own name, with your own email**, not your developer's

That last point matters. We often meet business owners whose domain was registered by a previous developer who has since disappeared, leaving them unable to move or renew their own website.

## What a domain costs

Domain prices vary by registrar and change with offers. Watch out for a cheap first year followed by a much higher renewal price, and check the renewal cost before you buy. A domain is a yearly cost, so turn on auto-renew so it never expires by accident.

## FAQ

**Is .in less trusted than .com?** Not for Indian customers. .in is widely used by Indian businesses, banks and government-related sites.

**Can I change from .in to .com later?** Yes, but it takes care: you need proper redirects so you don't lose Google rankings. It's easier to choose well at the start.

**Does .co.in rank differently from .in?** Both are under India's country domain. Choose the one that's available and easier to remember; .in is usually shorter.

**Does a new domain extension like .online hurt SEO?** Google says new extensions are treated like others for ranking, but customers may trust them less. For most businesses, .in or .com is the safer choice.
