To rank your business higher on Google Maps in Surat, you need a **verified, fully completed Google Business Profile** with the right category, a **steady flow of genuine reviews**, **accurate and consistent business details** everywhere online, and a **website that clearly mentions your services and your area**. Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, so your job is to be as relevant and prominent as possible ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).

When someone in Vesu searches "dentist near me" or someone in Varachha searches "CA near me", Google shows a map with three businesses. Those three get most of the calls. Here's how to become one of them.

## How Google decides who appears in Surat

1. **Relevance:** how well your profile matches the search. Categories, services and description all count.
2. **Distance:** how close you are to the person searching. A business in Adajan will naturally show more for searches made in Adajan than in Varachha.
3. **Prominence:** how well known and trusted you are: reviews, rating, links and mentions, and your website's strength.

You can't move your office, but you can win on relevance and prominence, which is why a well-run profile in Piplod can beat a neglected one next door.

## Step 1: Claim and verify your profile

Search your business name on Google Maps. If a listing exists, claim it; if not, create one at [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business/). Then verify it. Many businesses are now asked to verify by video, showing your signboard, location and proof you run the business. Until you're verified, you're unlikely to rank well.

## Step 2: Pick the right primary category

Your primary category is one of the strongest relevance signals. Be specific: "Dental clinic" rather than "Doctor", "Chartered accountant" rather than "Consultant", "Textile mill" or "Saree shop" rather than "Store". Add secondary categories only for things you genuinely do.

## Step 3: Fill in every section

- Business name exactly as on your signboard, with no added keywords ("Shah Dental Clinic", not "Shah Dental Clinic Best Dentist Surat")
- Address with landmark, matching your GST and bills
- Phone number that's answered during business hours
- Hours, including festival holidays like Diwali
- Services, each with a short description
- Your website link
- A description saying what you do, for whom, and where in Surat

## Step 4: Add real photos regularly

Photos of your shop front, signboard, interior, team and work help customers recognise you and choose you. Add new photos every month or two instead of uploading everything once.

## Step 5: Build reviews, steadily and honestly

Reviews are one of the factors Google mentions for local ranking, and they're what people read before calling.

- **Ask every customer**, ideally right after a good experience
- **Make it easy:** share your review link or QR code on WhatsApp, on the counter and on invoices
- **Reply to every review**, positive and negative, politely
- **Never buy reviews or offer discounts for them.** Google's policies prohibit it, and fake reviews can get removed or your profile restricted

Reviews in Gujarati or Hindi are just as valuable as English ones.

## Step 6: Keep your details consistent everywhere

Your business name, address and phone number should match exactly on your Google profile, website, Justdial, IndiaMART, Facebook, Instagram and any directories. Mismatched details (old phone numbers, different spellings) confuse customers and Google.

## Step 7: Make your website support your profile

Your profile links to your website, and Google uses your website to understand your business. Make sure it:

- Mentions your services and your area in Surat in plain words
- Has a page for each main service
- Shows your address and phone number, matching your profile
- Loads quickly on mobile and has a direct call button

A profile with a strong website usually outranks a profile with no website or a weak one.

## Step 8: Post updates and answer questions

Use Google Business Profile posts for offers, events and news, and add answers to common questions. It keeps your profile active and gives customers more reasons to choose you.

## Why your business might not show at all

If you can't find your business on Maps, the problem is usually verification, a suspended profile, a duplicate listing or wrong categories. Our guide to [why your business isn't showing on Google Maps](/insights/why-is-my-business-not-showing-on-google-maps) covers each one.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to rank on Google Maps in Surat?** It depends on your category and competition. Fixing your profile can show results within weeks; competitive categories in busy areas take longer.

**Can I rank in every area of Surat?** Distance matters, so you'll rank most strongly near your location. A strong profile and website can widen the area where you appear.

**Should I add "Surat" or keywords to my business name?** No. Use your real business name. Adding keywords breaks Google's guidelines and can get your profile suspended.

**Do I need a website to rank on Google Maps?** Not strictly, but a good website helps Google trust and understand your business, and helps customers decide.
