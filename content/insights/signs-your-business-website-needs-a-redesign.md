Your business website needs a redesign if it **looks dated next to competitors, doesn't work well on phones, loads slowly, doesn't bring in enquiries, can't be updated easily, or no longer describes what your business actually does**. If more than three of the signs below apply to you, a redesign will usually pay for itself faster than patching the old site.

Most of the businesses we work with in Surat already have a website. The problem isn't that they don't have one; it's that the website quietly makes them look smaller, older or less professional than they really are. Here's how to tell.

## The 12 signs

### 1. It looks dated next to your competitors

Open your website and your top three competitors' websites on your phone. If yours looks older, customers notice the same thing in seconds. For a buyer who doesn't know you, your website is often their first impression of your business.

### 2. It doesn't work properly on phones

Most visitors now arrive on a phone. Tiny text, buttons too close together, sideways scrolling or a menu that doesn't open all lose customers immediately.

### 3. It loads slowly

If your site takes several seconds to show anything, many visitors leave before it appears. Test it with Google's free [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/).

### 4. It doesn't bring enquiries

Visitors come, but nobody calls, messages or fills in the form. That usually means unclear messaging, weak calls to action, or no trust signals. See [why most business websites don't generate leads](/insights/why-most-business-websites-dont-generate-leads-and-how-to-fix-it-in-2026).

### 5. No direct call or WhatsApp option

If customers have to copy your number or hunt for contact details, you're losing enquiries. Our guide to [adding a WhatsApp button](/insights/add-whatsapp-button-to-website) shows how simple this is to fix.

### 6. It doesn't show up on Google

If you can't find your own business for your main services, or you only appear when searching your exact name, your website isn't helping you get found. See [why your website isn't getting traffic](/insights/why-is-my-website-not-getting-traffic-10-reasons-and-how-to-fix-them).

### 7. Your business has changed, but the website hasn't

New services, new products, a bigger team, a new office or new markets, and the website still describes the business you were five years ago.

### 8. You can't update it yourself

Every small change needs the developer, who takes weeks or has disappeared. A modern website should let you update text, photos and posts easily, or come with reliable support.

### 9. It's full of stock photos

Generic smiling people and handshake images make you look like every other business. Real photos of your team, premises and work build trust.

### 10. Visitors can't tell what you do in five seconds

If your homepage opens with "Welcome to our website" or a vague slogan, visitors leave confused. The first screen should say what you do, who it's for and what to do next.

### 11. Security warnings or broken features

"Not secure" warnings in the browser, broken forms, missing images, or outdated plugins are red flags for customers and for Google.

### 12. You're embarrassed to share it

If you hesitate to send your website link to a big client, that's the clearest sign of all.

## Fix or redesign?

Not every problem needs a full redesign.

| If your problem is mainly | Consider |
| --- | --- |
| A few outdated pages or photos | Updating content |
| Slow speed on an otherwise good site | Performance fixes |
| Weak Google presence | SEO and Google Business Profile work |
| Poor mobile layout, dated design, no enquiries | A redesign |
| Can't update it, broken platform, security issues | A rebuild on a better platform |

As a rule of thumb, if your site has more than three or four of the signs above, patching it costs more over time than starting fresh.

## How to plan a redesign that brings results

1. **Start with goals:** more calls, more quote requests, more export enquiries? Decide what success means.
2. **Keep what works:** check which pages already get Google traffic so you don't lose rankings.
3. **Redirect old pages** to their new addresses so links and rankings carry over.
4. **Rewrite the content for customers**, not just reshuffle the old text.
5. **Use real photos** of your business.
6. **Make contacting you effortless** from every page.
7. **Own everything:** domain, hosting, code and accounts in your name.

## FAQ

**How often should a business website be redesigned?** There's no fixed rule. Many businesses refresh every few years, or whenever the business changes significantly or the site stops performing.

**Will a redesign hurt my Google rankings?** It can, if old pages disappear without redirects. Planned properly, a redesign usually improves rankings.
