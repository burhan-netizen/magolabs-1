If your CA firm's website is not getting enquiries, the cause is usually one of four things: **visitors cannot quickly tell what you do and who you help, there is nothing that builds trust, the way to contact you is hidden or awkward, or the site is not built for phones**. Fix the structure and the path to contact, and the same traffic starts turning into conversations.

**MNP & Co.**, a growing accounting practice, had a website that technically existed but **barely brought in any enquiries**. We rebuilt it from scratch with a cleaner layout, a much stronger structure and a clear focus on turning visitors into enquiries. Here is what usually goes wrong, and what we changed.

## A website that exists is not the same as one that works

Many firms tick the "we have a website" box and move on. The site has a homepage, an about page and a list of services. But it does not do the job a website is supposed to do for a growing practice: bring in new clients.

For MNP & Co., the old site was online, but it was not working. It was a page online for the sake of it.

## Why CA firm websites fail to bring enquiries

### 1. Visitors cannot tell what you do in five seconds

If the first screen shows a generic slogan and a stock photo, visitors have to dig to understand your services. Most will not. The first screen should say plainly who you are, what you do and who you help.

### 2. Nothing builds trust

Clients are trusting you with their finances. They want to see the partners, their qualifications, years of practice and the kinds of clients you serve. Without that, the site gives them no reason to choose you over the next firm.

### 3. Contacting you is hard work

A contact page with a long form, an email address and nothing else loses people. Visitors expect to call, WhatsApp or send a short enquiry in one tap, from any page.

### 4. It is not built for phones

Most first visits happen on a mobile. Tiny text, slow loading and buttons that are hard to tap send visitors away before they read anything.

## What we changed for MNP & Co.

We **rebuilt the site from scratch** rather than patching the old one:

- **A cleaner layout**, so the firm's expertise and services are obvious within the first scroll.
- **A much stronger structure**, with services organised the way clients think about them and a clear path through the site.
- **Conversion focus throughout**, with an enquiry form and contact options placed where a ready visitor needs them, not hidden at the bottom of one page.

Everything on the new site is focused on one outcome: turning visitors into actual enquiries.

## The result

Since the rebuild, MNP & Co. has noticed **a real difference in how prospective clients respond after visiting the site**, exactly the kind of result a website revamp should deliver. In the firm's words, the old site barely got them any enquiries, and the rebuild changed that.

## Check your own site in five minutes

- Open it on your phone. Can you tell what the firm does within five seconds?
- Can you see who the partners are and their experience?
- Can you call, WhatsApp or enquire in one tap from the homepage?
- Does each main service have a clear explanation?
- When did the site last bring in an enquiry?
