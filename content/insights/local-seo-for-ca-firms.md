Local SEO for a CA firm means **showing up when people nearby search for the services you offer**, like "CA near me", "GST registration in Surat" or "income tax filing consultant". It comes down to a well-structured website with a page for each service, consistent location details, a complete Google Business Profile, and steady reviews from real clients.

We put this into practice for **K.D. Mayani & Co.**, a growing chartered accountancy practice that had been operating without a website. We built a structured, professional site with **local SEO** and a direct enquiry form. Here is what local SEO for a CA firm involves, step by step.

## Why local SEO matters for accountants

Most people choose an accountant close to home or work. When they search, Google shows a small map with a handful of firms, followed by websites. The firms in those spots get the calls. A practice without a website, or with one Google cannot understand, rarely appears, however good its work.

For K.D. Mayani & Co., the missing website meant missing the chance to **build authority with prospective clients before the first conversation**, at exactly the stage the practice was growing.

## Local SEO for CA firms, step by step

### 1. A website Google can read

Start with a clean, fast, well-structured site. Each page should have a clear title and heading that says what it is about and where you are. We built the K.D. Mayani site around what a CA firm needs to communicate: **credibility, clarity and ease of contact**, with a structure search engines can follow.

### 2. A page for each main service

One "Services" page listing everything gives Google very little to go on. Separate pages for your main services, such as income tax, GST, audit and company registration, each mentioning your city, give you a real chance of ranking for those searches.

### 3. Your location, consistent everywhere

Your firm's name, address and phone number should be exactly the same on your website, your Google Business Profile and any directory listings. Google uses this consistency to trust that your business is real and where it says it is.

### 4. A complete Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is what appears on the map. Choose the right category, add your services, hours and real photos, and keep it active. If your firm does not appear at all, our guide on [why a business does not show on Google Maps](/insights/why-is-my-business-not-showing-on-google-maps) walks through the usual causes. We also offer [Google Business Profile setup and optimisation](/services/google-business-profile).

### 5. Genuine reviews, steadily

Reviews affect where you rank on the map and whether people click. Ask happy clients to review you, make it easy with a direct link, and keep it steady rather than in one burst. Keep it within ICAI guidelines, which our [CA website guidelines article](/insights/icai-website-guidelines-ca-firm-website) covers.

### 6. A simple way to get in touch

Ranking only matters if visitors contact you. A **direct enquiry form**, a click-to-call number and WhatsApp turn search visibility into actual clients.

## Common local SEO mistakes CA firms make

**Different details in different places.** A slightly different address or phone number on your website, profile and directories confuses Google. Keep them identical.

**The wrong Google category.** Choosing a broad or wrong category on your Google Business Profile can keep you out of the searches that matter.

**No service pages.** Without pages for your main services, Google has little reason to show your site for searches like "GST registration in Surat".

**Asking for reviews only once.** A burst of reviews followed by nothing looks odd. Make asking a steady habit.

**Ignoring the profile after setup.** An inactive profile with old hours and no photos loses ground to firms that keep theirs current.

**Expecting results overnight.** Local SEO builds over weeks and months. Firms that keep going steadily usually overtake those that give up early.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does local SEO take for a CA firm?

Setting up your profile and fixing your website can improve local visibility within weeks. Ranking for competitive searches in a big city usually takes a few months of steady work.

### Do Google reviews help a CA firm rank higher?

Yes. The number, quality and recency of reviews all affect how you appear on Google Maps. Ask happy clients steadily, and stay within ICAI guidelines.

### Can a CA firm rank without a website?

A Google Business Profile alone can appear on the map, but a website with clear service pages makes you far more likely to rank and to be chosen.

### What is a citation in local SEO?

Any mention of your firm's name, address and phone number on another website, such as a business directory. Consistent citations help Google trust your location.
