A website for a loan advisor or DSA firm must **look established, speak plainly about money, and serve two very different visitors**: borrowers who need funding, and partners who could send you business. Calculators, a clear view of the people behind the firm and a simple way to talk to an expert turn a nervous visitor into an enquiry.

**Centurion Strategic Ventures** is a loan advisory and debt syndication firm in Ahmedabad, led by directors with **20+ years inside India's leading banks and NBFCs**. They were launching a brand new name. We built their website from the ground up. Here is what it needed, and what any finance or loan advisory firm can learn from it.

## The problem: new name, crowded market

Loan advisors are everywhere, and most of their websites look the same: stock photos, long lists of loan types, and big promises about approval. A new firm has no reputation online yet, so it has to look credible from the very first day.

Centurion had a second challenge. The site needed to speak to **borrowers** looking for funding and to **DSA partners** who might refer clients. Put both on one generic page and neither feels spoken to.

## What a loan advisory website needs

### 1. Position as a partner, not another agent

We positioned Centurion as **strategic finance partners** rather than another loan agent. The copy stays calm and free of jargon, and reveals detail gradually instead of dumping every product on the homepage. In finance, a quiet, confident tone builds more trust than bold claims.

### 2. Separate paths for borrowers and partners

Borrowers and partners have different questions, so they get their own clear path through the site and **their own enquiry flow, each going to its own inbox**. Borrower enquiries reach the right person fast, and partner conversations do not get lost among loan requests.

### 3. Calculators that answer the first question

Before a borrower talks to anyone, they want to know: can I afford this, and am I likely to qualify? We built **EMI and eligibility calculators** into the site. A visitor can check their numbers in a few taps, which builds confidence and brings in better-qualified enquiries.

### 4. The people behind the firm, front and centre

Experience is the strongest asset a new advisory firm has. A **directors section** puts their banking and NBFC background front and centre, so a visitor understands within seconds that this is a team that knows how lenders think.

### 5. A fast route to a real expert

Finance questions feel personal and often urgent. A **WhatsApp shortcut** alongside the enquiry forms lets a borrower reach an expert without filling in anything long.

### 6. Fast and premium on a phone

Most borrowers start on their phone. The site is built to load fast and feel premium on a small screen, because a slow, cluttered page makes any firm look careless with money.

## A quick checklist for your loan advisory website

- Does your site speak separately to borrowers and partners?
- Can a visitor estimate their EMI or eligibility without calling?
- Are the people behind the firm, and their experience, clearly shown?
- Is the copy calm and free of jargon and big promises?
- Can someone reach you on WhatsApp in one tap?

## Common mistakes on loan advisory websites

**Promising approvals.** "Guaranteed loan approval" or "lowest rates in India" sounds desperate and can mislead. Calm, accurate language builds more trust and attracts better clients.

**Listing every loan product on the homepage.** Twenty loan types in a grid overwhelm the visitor. Lead with who you help, then let people go deeper.

**No faces behind the firm.** In finance, experience is the selling point. A site that hides who runs the firm gives borrowers no reason to trust it with their details.

**Asking for too much information too soon.** Long forms asking for income, PAN and loan history put people off. Start with a short enquiry and collect details on the call.

**One path for everyone.** Borrowers and referral partners need different information. Mixing them confuses both.

**Ignoring mobile.** Most borrowers search on their phone. Calculators and forms must work perfectly on a small screen.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should a DSA or loan consultant website include?

A clear statement of who you help, the types of funding you arrange, the people behind the firm and their experience, simple calculators, and separate enquiry options for borrowers and partners.

### Are EMI calculators worth adding to a loan advisor website?

Yes. Borrowers want to check affordability before they talk to anyone. A calculator answers that first question and brings in enquiries from people who are better prepared.

### How do we make a new loan advisory firm look established?

Lead with the experience of the people behind it, use calm and accurate language, keep the design clean and premium, and make it easy to reach a real expert.

### Can borrower and partner enquiries go to different people?

Yes. In the project above, each enquiry flow goes to its own inbox, so borrower requests reach the right person quickly and partner conversations do not get lost.
